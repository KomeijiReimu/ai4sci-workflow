# Research for a target venue

Start where the author's request starts. For a topic recommendation, finish after the question review. For a full study, continue through the applicable decisions below. When the author brings finished results or a manuscript, review the existing evidence and do the requested work from there. Do not invent earlier decisions or retrospectively approve a plan.

## Who does what

The lead agent assigns the work. With omo-slim, use two Explorer sessions or equivalent readers for the initial searches. Use Oracle, or an available specialist configured for strong scientific reasoning, to challenge decisions. Choose from the host's roles, models and permissions; an agent name on its own does not identify a model. The analyst runs the comparisons, the writer prepares the manuscript, and a reviewer who did neither checks the finished work. Give reviewers the brief and evidence, not a requested verdict.

| Decision | What the lead sends and what the reviewer must establish |
|---|---|
| Recommend a question | In parallel, one reader checks recent accepted work in the intended track **and** looks across venues for a result that might already answer the question. The other checks the actual data, comparisons and cost. Send their sources, the leading question, its strongest alternative and proposed first test to the scientific reviewer. Ask which prior result or alternative explanation most threatens the proposed contribution. If it holds, change the question or advance a better candidate; have the reviewer check the replacement before recommending it. |
| Start the formal experiment | Before freezing the plan and committing the main compute budget, let the reviewer examine the claim, data split, strongest feasible baseline, metric and rival predictions. Ask what a positive result could still fail to distinguish. Resolve material objections in the design. The experimenter then freezes and executes the agreed comparison. |
| Change direction | Return to the same reviewer when an observation changes the main claim, comparison or use of the remaining budget. Send the relevant result rows and competing interpretations. Decide whether to run a discriminating control, narrow the claim or stop. Leave routine searches, plots and file edits with their owners. |
| Deliver the manuscript | Give a strong independent reviewer the exact manuscript or rendered package, data, run records, source passages and open objections. If the proposed reviewer materially shaped the design, use a fresh qualified reviewer session regardless of role name. If none is available, independent final review remains pending. Have the reviewer recompute the decisive result where possible and inspect the claims in the file to be delivered. Send substantive defects to their owner for repair and the changed version back to the reviewer who raised them. Close on that version. |

**What counts as reviewed.** The lead makes the child calls and reads their returned findings before making the corresponding decision. In the study's existing handoff or review note, record the actual role and host session or task reference, the version inspected (file and digest, versioned passage, or exact proposed question), the key objection or check and its disposition. For a chat-only recommendation, report this briefly without creating a study. Check the reference against the host's returned task and findings, including on resume. A planned assignment, a role typed into a note or an offline `audit` pass is not a completed review. If a reviewer cannot run, try an appropriate available substitute. Otherwise label the affected stage self-checked and its independent review pending. Keep a material objection open until the reviewer checks the repair or the narrower claim; a return message alone does not clear it.

**What the author gets.** Recommend a specific question, redesign it, or retire it on the evidence. Explain which paper, data check or objection determined the choice, what the first comparison would separate and what outcome would change the decision. For a manuscript, give the file, the scientific finding and any material issue still open. Address the research decision directly rather than speculating about acceptance.
