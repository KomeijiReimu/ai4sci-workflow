# Front-runner evidence register

The [evaluation protocol](protocol.md) defines what a peer comparison can establish. The five names below are **configurations**, not interchangeable representatives of a product family. The [pinned leaderboard](https://github.com/InternScience/ResearchClawBench-Home/blob/6be2ab8ca379bd3ad0c1e83d86c985e11af972af/data/leaderboard.json) gives 40 scores for each. All 200 [task records](https://github.com/InternScience/ResearchClawBench-Home/blob/6be2ab8ca379bd3ad0c1e83d86c985e11af972af/data/leaderboard_only_runs.json) have `details_exported: false`. A separate archive can still supply evidence for a run with a matching ID.

| Configuration | Observed work available for inspection | What can be compared |
|---|---|---|
| Qiushi Engine | The team's [benchmark report](https://oxelra.com/news/qiushi-engine-researchclawbench-report-zh-20260911.pdf) gives case excerpts. A [separate optical study](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.27092) describes adapting a protocol to a platform, measurement and a reviewer-triggered follow-up. Its [data deposit](https://zenodo.org/records/19890402) identifies reports, measurements, figures and scripts, but excludes the engine core and hardware-control software. | A useful example of criticism changing the next experiment. The independent optical work is not the benchmark run. A description and a deposit listing are not an independent physical reproduction or evidence of an identical software configuration. |
| InnoClaw 2.0 | The [research execution workspace](https://github.com/SpectrAI-Initiative/InnoClaw/commit/3663f42573c9ff5c1760fd28279e1449e31b05c7) and [final-report implementation](https://github.com/SpectrAI-Initiative/InnoClaw/commit/4a2a672346ce204c42b44349874f9c4a8335c060) are inspectable. | Compare checkpoint and reporting mechanisms. Neither commit establishes the version of the 2.0 entry or shows an actual investigator reproducing the instance that earned the score. |
| Ligase | The [product description](https://gradence.com.cn/products/biopharma.html) and [GEAR report](https://gradence.com.cn/reports/gear.html) describe roles and scientific tools. | The sources located do not include a version-linked, inspectable study with inputs, measured results and final manuscript. The corresponding scientific outcomes are unknown. |
| AutoSciRub | The [pinned external dataset](https://huggingface.co/datasets/huminclu/autoscirub-rcb-main-exp/tree/6a412f394d44b021d26d548d943a4106ff5e1c09/workspaces-leaderboard0903/Earth_001_20260831_143622) includes `_meta.json`, `report/report.md`, `outputs/initial_rubric.json`, `outputs/refined_rubric.json`, `outputs/reproduction_manifest.json` and `outputs/report_rubric_check.json`. | We joined `Earth_001_20260831_143622` to the official row: score 47.06, Codex + AutoSciRub, `gpt-5.6-sol`, 2,035 seconds. We opened the metadata, study, reproduction manifest and verification record. The manifest identifies the program, input digest and generated tables and figures. The second verification record reports a completed repair. The study characterizes reporting patterns and does not infer intervention efficacy. This is observed design, execution evidence, reporting and repair, not independent recovery of the primary results or proof that the agent originated the assigned research question. |
| EvoLab | Its [research guide](https://evo-lab.org/document/research/), [evolution guide](https://evo-lab.org/document/evolution/) and [OpenEvo naming history](https://evo-lab.org/blog/researchclawbench-first-place/) describe learning from one run for another. | Compare documented cross-session behavior. An example research prompt is not a completed study. Do not substitute results from EvoScientist for EvoLab. |

**Artifact checks.** For AutoSciRub, the 13 counts in the [archived state summary](https://huggingface.co/datasets/huminclu/autoscirub-rcb-main-exp/resolve/6a412f394d44b021d26d548d943a4106ff5e1c09/workspaces-leaderboard0903/Earth_001_20260831_143622/outputs/state_summary.csv) total 832 reported records. The three largest counts total 487, reproducing the report's 58.5% from its derived table. For Qiushi, we opened the reproducibility guide. The bounded download of the larger supplement timed out before the selected study report could be extracted. No measurements or figure inside that archive were independently verified here.

One of the five has an exact-run external workspace among the materials located. The others have differing amounts of product or family-level evidence. The [assessment](assessment.md) records the dimension-by-dimension comparison and the disposition of the proposed external trials. The [workbench and specialist review](peers.md) addresses which tool fits a researcher's task.
