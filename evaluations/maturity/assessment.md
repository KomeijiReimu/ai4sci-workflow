# Research workflow assessment, 24 November 2026

**Implementation accepted. Release not authorized.** The [protocol](protocol.md) requires evidence of comparable or stronger scientific work on all six dimensions for the same three or more of five named configurations. The coordinator and independent assessor (Oracle) recorded their decisions before exchanging reasons. Both found **0 of 5 comparisons fully substantiated**. This counts complete comparisons, not the capability of any system. The decision authorizes no release or push, including changes outside the homepages.

## What the studies demonstrate

The electricity study and penguin revision are public development cases. Both show how an investigator handled a specific research decision. Neither was administered as a fresh held-out trial.

| Research decision | Work inspected | Finding |
|---|---|---|
| Choose a question | The [idea workflow](../../skills/ai4sci-idea/SKILL.md) asks for the closest published result, usable data and a test on which rival explanations predict different outcomes. | This procedure is implemented. The electricity question and the penguin objection were already specified. Neither study tests how often a researcher starting with a broad topic would choose a worthwhile question. |
| Read the relevant evidence | The [climate note](../../examples/climate-sources/research/paper.md) distinguishes WMO's multi-dataset estimate from the Copernicus ERA5 estimate. The [load study](../../examples/power-forecast/research/review.md) cites the original paper for the weekly baseline and describes its own choice of local time for clock changes. | A reader can check the source passage and tell which decision the study made itself. The paired reading test used supplied passages, so it does not measure search recall. |
| Choose the next experiment | The German day-ahead forecast had lower error overall, but higher error in the hours of highest realized demand. The analyst [recorded the next comparison](../../examples/power-forecast/research/followup-decision.md) before measuring it: select the high hours using the forecasts rather than the observations. | The alternative selection retained 670 of the original 876 high hours. The weekly baseline still had lower error in this group. Both comparisons use 2019, so the second result addresses sensitivity to the definition of high demand. |
| Reproduce the calculation | A cold replay regenerated [four load-study outputs](../../examples/power-forecast/), and a [separate SQLite calculation](recheck_power.py) checked the reported contrasts against the official hourly CSV. | Across 8,756 paired hours, the day-ahead forecast's mean absolute error was 540 MW lower than that of the weekly baseline. Its error was 159 MW higher among the hours of highest realized demand and 161 MW higher among the hours selected by the forecasts' midpoint. Each difference compares group means. |
| Revise the delivered paper | The [penguin response](../../examples/penguins-revision/research/response.md) adds the training median as a control for absolute error. A [separate reviewer](../../examples/penguins-revision/research/independent-review.md) recalculated all predictions and inspected both pages of the actual Word document. | The fitted line's advantage narrowed from 384.707 g against the training mean to 372.361 g against the training median. All eight registered claims and the reused-holdout qualification reached the final file. The original study remained intact. |
| Hand off the study | The [final delivery](../../examples/penguins-revision/research/final-delivery.md) links the Word paper, evidence review, dated approval and author-stage handoff. The original files and the author's revision files matched their recorded digests in a clean checkout. | An independent investigator reconstructed predictions and errors from the measurements without the author's program. This tests handoff for recalculation. Continuing an unfamiliar open-ended investigation without the author was not tested here. |

## Trial disposition

No formal study was started or scored in this assessment.

Recovering posterior means for wage and sports data using the models and data supplied by [posteriordb](https://github.com/stan-dev/posteriordb/tree/91027f9cdd13b7e76283fec3f2898f59d4b83ec4) would test numerical portability. It would not test whether an investigator can choose a research question or revise a manuscript after a substantive objection.

[MLR-Bench](https://github.com/chchenhui/mlrbench) provides public workshop directions. [F1000Research](https://f1000research.com/for-authors/article-guidelines-new-versions) preserves public article versions and reviews. A future investigation can sample a rights-cleared direction and a separate material revision from these sources, issue only the permitted inputs, and withhold the later article until grading. Running it would strengthen the evidence about this workflow. Independently inspectable research from the named peer configurations would still be needed for the majority comparison.
