# External research assignments: prospective grading plan

This plan covers two research assignments: choosing a testable question from an unfamiliar direction, and responding to a material objection to an existing paper in a different domain. It was written before either assignment was drawn or issued. It supplements the [maturity protocol](../protocol.md). The [previous decision](../assessment.md) stands. Neither a local grade nor a score obtained with a substitute benchmark judge is a ResearchClawBench result.

## Evidence to issue and withhold

| Assignment | Operator receives | Grader inspects after submission |
|---|---|---|
| Research direction | The external research brief, permitted original readings with section locators, and data or an executable simulation specification. The brief must leave a meaningful choice of question and comparison. | The closest verified precedent, the rival predictions, the analysis actually run, its result and the resulting decision. An independently proposed, well-supported alternative can earn full credit. Agreement with the grader's favorite question earns none by itself. |
| Manuscript revision | The original submitted version, a genuine substantive reviewer comment, and the inputs or original results needed to address it. | The untouched original, the response, the manuscript returned for delivery and the evidence for changing or retaining the finding. Compare with the subsequently published response or revision only after both submissions are locked. The authors' response is a comparator, not an answer key. A justified rejection of the reviewer's suggestion can be correct. |

Before the draw, the custodian fixes the eligible population, reuse terms, exclusions, independent public draw and reserve order. Before disclosing the selected prompt, verify the actual operator cannot read the checkout, prior attempts, reference responses or grading files. Supply each permitted file individually through a read-only mount. Publication on the internet is compatible with withholding a reference from the operator in this trial. It is not proof of absence from model training.

## Grade the work, not its file count

Each applicable indicator is scored 0, 1 or 2. Award **2** when the full criterion is verified. Award **1** for a verifiable partial result and identify the missing part. The table gives examples. Award **0** if no substantive part is supported or if the evidence contradicts the decisive claim for that indicator. Record the reason and the source passage, input row, calculation or manuscript passage behind every grade. Restating an instruction, passing the kit's own audit or agreeing with the original authors does not by itself earn credit.

| Indicator | Example of 1: partial evidence | Evidence required for 2 |
|---|---|---|
| Question choice (direction only) | The operator identifies a question left open by the closest source but does not identify a feasible test that distinguishes explanations. | The operator chooses a question the brief did not dictate, identifies what the closest source already answered and names a feasible observation on which plausible explanations differ. A reproduction can qualify if the choice and its scientific value are explained. |
| Reading (both) | An original passage supports the cited result, but the submission leaves a material limit on its use unexplained. | Original passages support the decisive interpretation. The operator distinguishes source-reported results from new calculations and identifies what the supplied reading cannot establish. |
| Design (both) | The baseline and metric address the question, but the submission leaves an exclusion that affects the comparison unexplained. | The baseline, comparison, unit and exclusions address the chosen question or objection. The treatment of reused evidence, selection and uncertainty fits the inference being made. |
| Execution and replication (both) | A second investigator recovers a component, such as the baseline estimate, but cannot reconstruct the decisive contrast. | A second investigator obtains the decisive quantity from the permitted inputs, not the submitted summary. The direction, units and scope agree with the delivered conclusion at justified precision. If the objection needs no new experiment, verify why the existing evidence answers it and recompute the decisive result from that evidence. |
| Interpretation and adaptation (both) | The manuscript describes the result accurately but does not explain the decision that followed. | The manuscript explains which predictions the evidence distinguishes, if any, and which explanations remain possible. Following up, narrowing the claim, stopping or retaining a result must follow from the observation. A negative or inconclusive result can earn full credit when the conclusion and decision fit what the test could resolve. |
| Revision (revision only) | The reply addresses the objection, but the material qualification it relies on appears only in the letter, not in the manuscript. | The response identifies the objection and locates the relevant passages in the delivered manuscript. Any necessary correction reaches all affected text, tables and figures. If the original finding stands, verify its support and scope in the returned manuscript and the reason for retaining it in the response. If no edit is warranted, the explicitly identified original may be returned unchanged. Credit this as handling the objection, not as making a correction. |
| Handoff (both) | A fresh investigator can identify the analysis and its inputs, but the instructions do not identify which of two outputs belongs to the delivered manuscript. | Without asking the author, a fresh investigator uses the delivered instructions to locate the correct study state, inputs and result, reproduce that result and identify what remains open or why the work stopped. Grade the ability to resume from the package here. Agreement with an independent calculation is graded under execution. |

Report six grades per assignment. Mark revision **not applicable** on the direction assignment and question choice **not applicable** on the revision assignment. A case passes this local test only with 2 on all six applicable indicators. Report verified scientific findings even if the case misses that threshold. Fabricated evidence, access to withheld material or a final manuscript retaining a withdrawn claim invalidates the affected trial. A timeout is an observed outcome and is not replaced with a reserve. Record a preparation failure before scientific dispatch separately from a started attempt that returned no usable research.

## Roles, resource limits and decision

The custodian selects materials but does not solve or grade the tasks. Each operator starts from a fresh process with the frozen workflow kit, its permitted inputs and its own writable study. A numerical reviewer works from each submitted study and its original inputs. A separate reader checks source support and the manuscript. Share the grading results only after both submission inventories, including failed or empty attempts, have been locked. The coordinator and Oracle assess the same evidence independently.

The two primary attempts share a zero additional model-spend limit. Each has a maximum of two hours, with two hours of review in aggregate. Before launch, record the backend, available tools, step limit, wall-clock rule, resource limits and excluded services. After each attempt, record actual usage. These two assignments use the available CPU configuration. Do not extrapolate a restricted local operator's performance to a full OpenCode session with different models and collaborators. No scientific retry is automatic. Preserve the failed work if a later repair is authorized.

Two passing cases would demonstrate transfer on these assignments only. They do not count as comparisons with named leading configurations. The previous majority judgment and all release gates in the maturity protocol remain unchanged. This grading plan authorizes no software release or push.
