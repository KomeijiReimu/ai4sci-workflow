# Comparing research products on attributable work

**Prospective question.** On the computational assignments examined, does AI4Sci Workflow match or exceed the observed work of the same three or more peers across all six [research dimensions](protocol.md#decision-rule)? The five peers are Qiushi Engine, InnoClaw, Ligase, AutoSciRub and EvoLab. The comparison concerns identified investigations or newly pinned builds. The [historical configuration assessment](assessment.md) remains unchanged. A higher score with a different judge is not evidence for this comparison.

## Select investigations before reading their results

1. Retain all five identities, including products with unavailable research output. For published investigations, use the producers' official websites, repositories, linked academic papers and producer-deposited research archives available by 2026-11-23 23:59 UTC. Record the source URL, producer, date, version or deposit ID and task domain. Use metadata, titles and file inventories to screen for **benign non-biomedical computational work**. An advertisement, sample prompt or unexecuted example is not a study. Preserve incomplete and negative cases. If an otherwise eligible investigation's metadata does not reveal which research decisions it offered, keep it in the unknown-opportunity stratum. Apply the same selection cap to that stratum. Neither an unavailable file nor a finding made during inspection frees a slot for a replacement.
2. Distinguish two opportunities: a broad direction with room for the operator to choose a question, and a material scientific challenge requiring a response. Record which parts were supplied to the operator. An assigned question does not demonstrate original question selection. A study package tag identifies the research release, not automatically a build of the engine. The previously identified Qiushi `structural-study-2026-09-28.1` is a purposive candidate. The optical study and AutoSciRub `Earth_001_20260831_143622` already contributed to prior assessments. Explicitly mark prior exposure instead of treating these cases as newly discovered or blind.
3. Before inspecting new outcomes, freeze the deduplicated inventory for each product and stratum. Record the exact product names and stable case IDs used for the draw. Identify one future public beacon round and the field whose published text will supply the randomness. Select all cases if there are at most two. For a larger stratum, select the two cases with the smallest SHA-256 digests of the UTF-8 string `beacon randomness:product:stable case ID` with literal colons and no trailing newline. Save the published round value exactly as used, its source and the selection receipt with the frozen inventory so another reader can reproduce the draw offline. If the same investigation is selected in both opportunity strata, inspect it once and retain both labels. Document any part of the search for which complete enumeration was impossible. The resulting sample describes selected cases, not typical performance.
4. For a new run using a public build, register the exact product revision, research host, model, tools, inputs, operator help and resource ceiling before opening the assignment. Inspectable source is not a run. Do not start new competitor runs, contact maintainers, upload a private study, or incur fees without the necessary authorization and allocation. Accurately attribute operator involvement on every side, including our host stack. A single local model without collaborator roles tests only that configuration.

## Compare the research

For every prospective comparison, document the scientific demand on each side **before grading**. What did the researcher have to choose? Which rival or control mattered? What would a new investigator have to recompute, verify or change? Mark a dimension **not comparable** when the tasks make materially different scientific demands on it, such as discovering a mathematical construction versus calculating a simple error. Mark it **unknown** when the opportunity, attribution or evidence is insufficient to judge it. Preserve the selected case and its other dimensions in either event.

Use the following anchors on both sides:

| Dimension | Evidence to inspect |
|---|---|
| Question selection | A contemporaneous choice between viable questions or explanations, a check against the closest work, a falsifiable distinction, and who made the choice. |
| Grounded reading | The actual source and version, the decisive passage, the claim it supports and the acknowledged limit on the inference. |
| Design and adaptation | A control that distinguishes plausible alternatives, the observation that motivated the next decision, and the preserved original comparison. A justified stop can qualify. |
| Execution and replication | Recover the decisive quantity from inputs, or independently check a proof or certificate. Recompute the comparison at the same scientific level, not just a percentage from an already aggregated table. |
| Manuscript and revision | Read the criticism, result and actual manuscript returned. Identify how the interpretation changed, or how evidence supports retaining it. Record an observed correction separately from a justified decision not to edit. |
| Handoff | A separate investigator locates the current evidence, replays or verifies the result and identifies what can be done next from the delivered materials alone. |

Record which actions belong to the product, a researcher, a reviewer, or an unknown contributor. A verified mathematical construction or accurate manuscript does not establish who originated it. A human-supplied correction is not an agent-originated correction. A companion case can fill an opportunity gap only for the same configuration or a demonstrably continuous investigation.

## Decision and publication

Publish the record of every selected case, relevant receipt and dimension judgment, including failures, unknown authorship, unavailable products and mismatches. Respect restrictions on redistributing source materials. For each dimension, **below**, **comparable** and **stronger** describe **AI4Sci's work relative to the peer's work**, with evidence and a rationale. Count a peer once only if our work is comparable or stronger on all six dimensions against the same attributable peer configuration or a demonstrably continuous investigation. A **below**, **unknown** or **not comparable** cell leaves that peer uncounted. Unknown and not comparable are not losses for either side. All five peers remain in the denominator. More model calls or a more elaborate workflow earn no credit by themselves.

The coordinator and Oracle must independently reach the same judgment on the same three or more products before authorizing the scoped capability claim. This would not confer a leaderboard rank or establish historical parity. Engineering checks, the two [external transfer assignments](real-transfer/adjudication.md), and official same-task scores are distinct evidence. The two transfer attempts retain their existing limits. This protocol authorizes neither additional attempts nor paid judging. The two root homepage revisions remain outside any implementation push.
