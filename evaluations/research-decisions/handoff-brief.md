# An independent handoff exercise on a completed comparison

The recipient starts with the [archived branch study](../../examples/branch-decision/), the workflow checkout and the request below. The recipient did not write its program or its manuscript. This is a small integration check of the ordinary assisted workflow, separate from the CPU operator trial and the comparison with other products.

> A collaborator wants to cite this experiment as evidence that one method always yields the smallest transferable file. Recover the decisive comparison without using its encoder. Tell the collaborator what the available measurements support, identify one objection that could change the use of the result, and return a corrected or justified unchanged passage with a reply. Do not change the archive.

Review the actual returned files. Check whether the numbers were independently recovered from the input, whether the objection and response address the proposed claim rather than just repeating the audit, and whether the manuscript the recipient would send supports that reply. The recipient may use the original format or Markdown. They need not invent results on new populations or prepare a formal venue submission. A reasoned refusal to broaden the claim is an acceptable outcome.
