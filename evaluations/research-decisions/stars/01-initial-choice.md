# Two clocks for stellar variability: initial choice

25 November 2026 · First decision, before the targeted challenge. No time series or outcome tables analysed.

| Trigger | Candidate and first comparison | Decision it could change |
|---|---|---|
| A method for following a starspot has a built-in instrumental rival. | In short-period Kepler binaries, do primary and secondary minima wander in opposite directions because the light distribution changes, or because the detector samples a shifting part of each minimum? Compare the same eclipses in short- and long-cadence photometry. A resolved displacement should survive in the minute data; sampling alone should make it largely a long-cadence feature. [Tran et al. (2013), “O−C Eclipse Times,” Table 2](https://doi.org/10.1088/0004-637X/774/1/81) find anticorrelated timings and explicitly note cadence beats. [Conroy et al. (2014), §§2.4–2.5](https://arxiv.org/html/1306.0512v2#S2.SS4) report both spurious signals and limited short-cadence coverage. | Which apparent timing excursions merit follow-up as changes in the binary’s brightness profile? |
| Two rotation measurements disagree where a catalog has to assign a period. | For weakly modulated dwarfs with an autocorrelation period around 15 days, does rotation inferred from the splitting of acoustic modes favor that period or the gradient-of-power-spectrum estimate? A genuine surface period should broadly agree with a well-resolved seismic period; a reduction-induced peak need not. [Reinhold et al. (2023), §§4.1, 4.5, Table C.1](https://doi.org/10.1051/0004-6361/202346789) give the disagreement and selection rule; [Nielsen et al. (2015), §3, Table 1](https://www.aanda.org/articles/aa/full_html/2015/10/aa26615-15/aa26615-15.html) show what the two clocks measure in five favorable stars. | When should a rotation catalog keep the 15-day period rather than replace it? |

**Provisional choice: binary minima.** The observation can in principle be repeated at two resolutions in the same star *at the same time*. The [binary catalog dictionary](https://keplerebs.villanova.edu/about#OnlineCatalog) supplies KIC, period, epoch, morphology, and links by cadence; [Kepler DR25 nominal light curves](https://exoplanetarchive.ipac.caltech.edu/docs/Kepler_Data_Products_Overview.html) come by quarter (long cadence) or month (short cadence). The rotation option needs a rare intersection of weak variability, a measurable oscillation splitting, and two periods far enough apart for that splitting to distinguish them. Neither eligible sample size has been established. Before recommending binary timings, check whether a simultaneous comparison already exists, whether the time baseline is long enough, and whether agreement across cadences would actually establish a spot origin.
