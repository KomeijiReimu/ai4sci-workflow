- Requested deliverable and current status:
- Frozen question, comparison and amendments:
- Command, environment and actual input version:
- Raw output, figure generation and manuscript paths:
- Supported conclusions and their exact evidence:
- Negative outcomes, untested criteria and blocked dependencies:
- Review mode (separate reviewer or self-check), review file and unresolved findings:
- To reproduce or resume (next action and owner; do not repeat completed work):

For a submission or revision, also link the original sent version, reviewer concern, supplemental decision and results, response, and exact final file that was opened and checked. Identify anything still promised rather than completed.

When work splits across questions, experimental branches or review rounds, identify the current candidate or manuscript, its parent, and the observed reason to continue it. Point to alternatives that were ruled out, the reason an issue reopened, and what evidence would change the next decision. A single-pass study needs no extra records.

For concurrent tasks, list each active owner and their writable paths, including who will merge the manuscript and update this handoff. Clear or transfer assignments as tasks finish.

For publication-oriented work, link each applicable scientific decision to the returned review: role and host task reference, question or file version inspected, decisive finding and how it was resolved. Keep a self-check or a pending independent review labeled as such.

For a remote experiment package, link the issuing checkout/revision, frozen plan and delivered archive (SHA-256). Keep the three review references, actual model profiles, concrete findings, dispositions and host-check outcome outside the archive. State which prerequisites still need checking on the receiver and whether anything was run.
