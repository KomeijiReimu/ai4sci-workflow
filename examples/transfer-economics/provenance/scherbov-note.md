# Benchmarking a mortality measure

Scherbov, S., and Gietel-Basten, S., “Measuring inequalities of development at the sub-national level: From the human development index to the human life indicator,” [article](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0232014). Supplied publisher excerpt, CC BY. Source locator: Results and discussion, final paragraph (Fig. 6).

Brief excerpt: “We found that the correlation coefficient between HDI and HLI for 2016 was 0.87.”

Study note: The authors compared subnational development measures for US states. HLI uses the distribution of ages at death. Country-average life expectancy cannot recover that distribution. An association in levels also need not describe changes.
