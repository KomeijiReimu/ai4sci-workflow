# How warm was 2024?

## Estimate

For 2024, Copernicus reported 1.60°C of warming using ERA5, while the WMO consolidated six datasets and reported 1.55 °C (with a margin of uncertainty of ± 0.13 °C).

Copernicus used 1850-1900 as its estimate of the pre-industrial baseline.

## Interpretation

The WMO distinguishes long-term warming, measured over decades rather than an individual year, from this annual anomaly.

The two estimates describe the same year but come from different data products.

Sources: [Copernicus annual bulletin](https://climate.copernicus.eu/copernicus-2024-first-year-exceed-15degc-above-pre-industrial-level) and [WMO consolidated assessment](https://wmo.int/news/media-centre/wmo-confirms-2024-warmest-year-record-about-155degc-above-pre-industrial-level), both issued on 10 January 2025.
