## Response to General Comments, second bullet

25 November 2026 · Independent proposed derivative of the v1 preprint, not an author-approved response. Local Qwen drafted the response; the delivery coordinator verified and restored the original short-window evidence.

**Original 20-year comparison retained.** Table R1 now puts all four original Figure 4 windows (1900–1919, 1920–1939, 1940–1959, 2000–2019) beside the longer windows for every GAM. Its short-window cells are direct means of the v1 fitted annual rates. The separate note quotes the original TrNcZw rates and their original reported uncertainty; it does not assign newly calculated intervals to those 20-year cells. Original Table 2 retains the authors’ short-window null tests, not tests of our longer contrast.

**Whole period.** The overall rate of SLR is 2.04 [1.89, 2.22] mm/yr for Tr, 2.08 [1.94, 2.23] mm/yr for TrNc, 1.94 [1.78, 2.08] mm/yr for TrNcZw, and 1.92 [1.70, 2.07] mm/yr for TrNcPd.

**Longer windows.** The earlier period (1900–1959) shows lower rates (1.82 [1.51, 2.13] mm/yr for Tr, 1.85 [1.64, 2.12] mm/yr for TrNc, 1.66 [1.43, 1.81] mm/yr for TrNcZw, and 1.73 [1.44, 2.01] mm/yr for TrNcPd). The later period (1960–2019) shows higher rates (2.26 [1.87, 2.65] mm/yr for Tr, 2.30 [1.98, 2.64] mm/yr for TrNc, 2.23 [2.00, 2.47] mm/yr for TrNcZw, and 2.11 [1.72, 2.49] mm/yr for TrNcPd).

**Interpretation.** Table R1 provides the whole-period rates and the 1960–2019 minus 1900–1959 contrasts: +0.44, +0.45, +0.57 and +0.38 mm/yr for Tr, TrNc, TrNcZw and TrNcPd, respectively. The adjoining text and period-rates.csv report the sensitivity to moving the later window to 1970–2019. The original tests using 20-year comparisons are not applicable to the new fixed 60-year contrast, and these spread-only bands do not test statistical significance. They include zero for Tr and TrNcPd, but not for TrNc or TrNcZw. The amendment does not claim significance or causal inference.

The brackets in the **whole-period and longer-window paragraphs above** represent the conditional spread-only sensitivity bands defined in Table R1, not calibrated confidence intervals. No null model was supplied for this period contrast and no new p-value was calculated. The provisional design text mentioned p-values; we did not implement that unsupported rule.
