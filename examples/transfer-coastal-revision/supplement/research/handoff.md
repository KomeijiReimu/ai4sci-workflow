# Handoff: Dutch coastal period comparison

**Delivery.** [Editable whole manuscript](../../revised.md), [referee response](../../response.md), [line map](../../changes.md), [supplemental report](paper.md), [claim-to-data ledger](claims.json), [independent scientific review](../../independent-review.md). The requested comparison comes from RC1 p. 1, General Comments, bullet 2.

**Design and provenance.** The primary [plan](plan.json) was [frozen](plan.sha256) before computing the new period outcomes. The analysis aggregates the original v1 fitted curves, 100 trajectories per model and the observed gauge panel. The [input manifest](../inputs.json) pins the source objects and the [run record](run.json) pins each output. The unshifted result at the path specified in the plan is retained. The [separate sensitivity analysis](calibration-amendment.md) responds to the observed discrepancy between the fitted rates and the archive's ensemble location. No original GAM was refitted.

**Read the results.** In [summary.csv](summary.csv), `estimate` is the mean fitted rate (or difference of fitted means). `draw_median`, `lo95`, `hi95`, `lo90` and `hi90` are **unshifted** trajectory statistics and do not define a confidence interval about `estimate`. [period-draws.csv](period-draws.csv) retains the same-draw period values by model and draw ID. [centered-sensitivity.csv](centered-sensitivity.csv) gives the *same* point estimate and the **assumption-dependent** percentiles, along with `uncalibrated_draw_mean` and `additive_shift`; [centered-period-draws.csv](centered-period-draws.csv) gives those transformed summaries. Each window is defined in the plan or report, with units mm/yr throughout. [support.csv](support.csv) binds the four model time spans, draw counts, derivative check and inspected minimum years; [gauge-check.csv](gauge-check.csv) reports unadjusted slopes, not a fifth GAM.

**Interpretation.** Under both cutoff readings all fitted differences are positive. Only conditional on transfer of the archived scatter do the long-window intervals exclude zero for TrNcZw and narrowly for TrNc. Tr and TrNcPd remain inconclusive even then. No validated uncertainty for the sign of the fitted contrasts or date of onset follows. Additional evidence needed for that inference: v1 draw-generation and normalization documentation or correctly centered and scaled joint draws. The authors’ original short-window p-values are not new results.

**Reproduction.** The [commands](../../README.md#reproduce-or-verify) regenerate both analyses from the same digest-checked inputs. The numerical checker recomputes fitted period means from the boundary levels and recomputes the empirical percentiles from the individual annual trajectories.
