# Why the centered ranges are a sensitivity analysis

**What the original comparison found.** In none of the four models does the [central 95% range of the unshifted draw means](summary.csv) contain the whole-period fitted mean. For TrNcZw the fitted mean is 1.9414 mm/yr; the median across draws is 3.8680 mm/yr. The archived `rate_slr` agrees with the centered finite difference of the fitted `trend` in cm after conversion to mm/yr. Its short-window fitted means agree at published precision with Figure 4c. The supplied material does not establish the normalization of the draw files. Halving all draw values to correct an apparent location discrepancy would also halve their spread. The unscaled 5th–95th percentile widths for the first, third and last Figure 4c windows (0.83, 0.54 and 1.04 mm/yr) are closer to the reported widths (0.9, 0.7 and 1.1) than half those widths. That scale check does not validate coverage for the fitted rates.

**Additional comparison.** After discovering the location discrepancy, we specified a sensitivity analysis that uses the fitted rates for location and transfers the empirical ensemble scatter without rescaling. Within each model and year, subtract the ensemble mean from every draw and add the fitted rate. Equivalently, for any window or paired contrast with fitted estimate θ and original draw summaries bᵢ, take the linear empirical percentiles of θ + bᵢ − mean(b). An additive shift preserves the distribution's width and skew; centering by the ensemble *mean* commutes with time averaging and paired subtraction. There is no cross-model pairing. The [sensitivity results](centered-sensitivity.csv) report the unshifted ensemble mean and the shift for each row.

**Interpretation.** Both summaries use the same periods, fits, 100 draws per model, time pairing and empirical quantiles. Only the unshifted summary was specified in the [frozen design](plan.json). The additional centering assumes that the archived scatter and temporal covariance apply to fitted-rate errors. That assumption, and therefore the coverage for the fitted contrast, is unverified. Under this assumption the longer-window ranges include zero for Tr and TrNcPd, barely exclude zero for TrNc, and exclude zero for TrNcZw. Neither set of ranges dates the onset of acceleration.
